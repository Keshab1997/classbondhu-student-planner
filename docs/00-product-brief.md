# 00 — Product brief

## Vision

Make the everyday academic routine less stressful by putting a student's next class, attendance standing, and upcoming coursework in one quick, dependable place.

## Audience

- Primary: college and university students who manage several subjects and a weekly class schedule.
- Initial launch context: India, with Bengali, English, and Hindi UI.
- Age range is not finalized. Do not assume all users are adults; resolve the audience and advertising treatment before release.

## Core user problem

Students often track timetable, attendance, and assignment deadlines in separate places or from memory. Generic to-do apps do not explain subject attendance, while attendance-only apps often omit what is due today.

## Product promise

- A student can check today's plan at a glance.
- Attendance calculations are clear, editable, and based on recorded classes.
- Important deadlines can be captured quickly and reminded locally.
- Core features keep working without an internet connection.

## Success measures for a small beta

Measure only privacy-respecting, aggregate signals where possible. Early qualitative goals:
- A new user can add subjects and a timetable without help.
- A user can record a class in a few seconds.
- Users can explain why the attendance percentage changed.
- A deadline reminder fires at the configured time on supported devices.
- No data is lost after app restart or ordinary app update.

Do not add analytics until the data collection, consent, and privacy disclosures are explicitly designed.

## Positioning and name

- Working product name: **ClassBondhu**.
- Working listing title: **ClassBondhu: Student Planner**.
- Promise in plain language: “Routine, attendance, and deadlines—together.”
- A quick web search did not surface an exact Play Store listing for “ClassBondhu”; search results are not a guarantee of availability. Verify the final name, trademark, and store listing before publication.

## Non-goals for v1

- Social feed, public profiles, chat, or a notes marketplace.
- Automatic attendance from college systems, GPS, Bluetooth, or classroom scanning.
- Cloud backup, multi-device sync, or account sign-in.
- AI tutoring or automatic note summarization.
- Payments, subscriptions, or paid features.
- Ads that interrupt marking attendance or completing a task.
